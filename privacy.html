Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Home Assistant is a private application used by a single person, its owner, to manage their own Google account data.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app accesses Gmail messages and Google Calendar events of the owner's Google account, only to perform actions the owner requests.

Use of data

Google user data is used solely to provide the assistant functionality to the owner. It is not shared with third parties, sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models.

Storage

Access tokens are stored securely on the owner's own server. Email and calendar content is not retained beyond what is needed to carry out requests.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact the app owner through the GitHub profile hosting this page.
